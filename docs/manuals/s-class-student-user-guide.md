# S-Class Student User Guide

This guide explains the current S-Class student experience in simple,
step-by-step terms.

## Register

1. Open the S-Class website.
2. Select **Register**.
3. Enter your **First name**, **Last name**, **Email address**, **Mobile
   number**, **School**, and **School ID**.
4. Enter a **Password** and **Confirm password**.
5. Select **Create account**.
6. Check your email and select the confirmation link.

Your password must contain at least 8 characters, 1 uppercase letter, and 1
number. You may need to confirm your email before signing in.

If the confirmation email does not arrive, check your spam or junk folder and
use the available option to try again or return to the login page.

## Log in and log out

1. Open the S-Class login page.
2. Enter your **Email address** and **Password**.
3. Select **Log in**.

In the student Portal, select **Sign out** from the sidebar or footer when you
are finished. On the public website, the account menu may use **Log out**.

## Reset your password

1. Open the S-Class login page.
2. Select **Forgot password?**
3. Enter your registered email address.
4. Select **Send reset link**.
5. Check your email and open the password-reset link.
6. Enter and confirm your new password.
7. Sign in again with the new password.

If the email does not arrive, check your spam or junk folder and use the
available **Try again** option.

## Profile and password

1. Open **Profile**.
2. Update your first name, last name, mobile number, school, or school ID.
3. Select **Save changes**.

To change your password, use the **Account Security** section. Enter your new
password and confirmation, then select **Update password**. The password must
contain at least 8 characters, 1 uppercase letter, and 1 number.

## Dashboard

1. Open **Dashboard** from the Portal sidebar.
2. Select **Continue learning** to return to your current learning activity.
3. Select **View plans** to review Standard access options.
4. Review your saved subjects, lessons watched, quizzes taken, and recent quiz
   results.
5. Use **My Books** to review book orders when available.

The Dashboard gives you a summary of your learning activity and links to the
next useful action.

## Manage devices

1. Open **Profile**.
2. Select **Manage devices**.
3. On **Active devices**, find the device you no longer use.
4. Select **Sign out** beside that device.

S-Class currently allows one mobile device and one laptop or desktop device.
If you reach the device limit, sign out an old device before trying to use a
new one. Do not share your password.

## Free and Standard access

- **Free Preview** lessons are available without a Standard subscription when
  the lesson is marked for preview.
- Free video access is limited to a **30-second video preview**.
- Where applicable, Free access shows the **first five PDF pages**.
- Full videos, complete PDFs, Problem Sets, progress tracking, and answer
  review may require **Standard** access.
- **Standard** access is available while your paid subscription is active.
- Content cannot be downloaded.

Some content may have another valid access rule, such as a book-related
solution PDF. The lesson or book page will show the requirement.

A locked lesson cannot be opened until its access requirement is met.

## Find a subject and curriculum

1. Open **Subjects**.
2. Use the **Subject library** to browse or search.
3. Use **Search subjects…** to find a subject by name.
4. Select a subject to open its curriculum.
5. Use **My Subjects** for subjects you have saved and **All Subjects** to
   browse the full available list.
6. Choose a Week, then select an available Day lesson.

The curriculum shows lessons in Week/Day order. Some later lessons remain
locked until earlier lessons have been watched or completed.

### Enlarged Day-card preview

On a desktop or laptop, point to an available Day card to see an enlarged
lesson preview. The preview may show:

- the lesson's Preview Image;
- the Day number;
- the lesson title;
- useful lesson information such as duration, Video, Solutions, or watched
  status when available; and
- **View Lesson**.

If the lesson does not have a custom Preview Image, the standard S-Class logo
is shown instead. Select **View Lesson** to open the lesson using the normal
lesson access rules.

The enlarged preview is for desktop and laptop pointer interaction. Touch and
mobile users should continue to select a Day card normally; they do not need to
trigger a hover preview. Locked cards remain locked and do not become
accessible through the preview. Exam cards retain their normal curriculum
behavior.

## Free Preview lessons

Select a lesson marked **Free Preview** to view the content available to guests
or Free accounts. Free Preview access does not unlock other lessons or premium
content.

## Subscribe to Standard

1. Open **Subscription**.
2. Review the Free and Standard comparison.
3. Select the Standard duration you want.
4. Continue to the secure payment page.
5. Complete the payment.
6. Return to S-Class and wait for your access status to update.

If payment was completed but access has not updated, keep the payment details
and contact S-Class support. Do not submit repeated payments while the first
payment is being checked.

## Watch a lesson and mark it complete

1. Open an accessible lesson from the curriculum.
2. Select the lesson video.
3. Use the video controls to play, pause, and seek where available.
4. Watch at least 95% of the lesson video.
5. Select **Mark as Watched** when it becomes available.

Completion may be required before related Problem Sets or subsequent
curriculum content becomes available. A Free account may see only the
30-second preview instead of the full video.

## Reviewer and PDF content

1. Open the reviewer or PDF section inside an accessible lesson.
2. Follow the on-screen viewer controls.
3. If the content is limited or locked, review the access message shown on the
   page.

Free access may show only the first five pages, while Standard access may
provide the full permitted content. Some solution PDFs require the associated
book to be purchased. Content cannot be downloaded.

## Problem Sets and quizzes

1. Open the Problem Set attached to the lesson.
2. Read each question and choose an answer.
3. Submit the attempt when finished.
4. Review the result and any available explanations.
5. Open **Quiz History** to review earlier attempts and results.

Some Problem Sets are locked until you have Standard access or have completed
the related lesson. If a Problem Set is locked, follow the displayed upgrade
or completion instruction.

## Progress and completion

Your Dashboard and lesson pages show available progress information. Watching
the required portion of a lesson and selecting **Mark as Watched** updates the
curriculum state. Progress can affect whether a later sequential lesson or
related Problem Set becomes available.

## Books and book orders

1. Open **Books**.
2. Select a book to view its description, price, and availability.
3. Select the option to add it to checkout.
4. Enter the requested shipping and contact information.
5. Review the order details.
6. Complete the payment.
7. Keep the confirmation details for support or delivery questions.

After checkout, use **My Books** or select **View My Books** from the order
confirmation page to review your book orders.

Order statuses can include:

- **Pending**: the order is waiting for the next processing step.
- **Paid**: payment has been confirmed.
- **Shipped**: the package has been sent.
- **Delivered**: delivery has been recorded.
- **Cancelled**: the order is no longer being processed.

## Opening a lesson

Select an accessible Day card directly to open a lesson. Locked Day cards
remain unavailable until the displayed access or completion requirement is met.
